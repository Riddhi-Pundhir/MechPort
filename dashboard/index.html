KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS & DRIVING GUIDE

GUIDE

Driving Controls

  • W or ↑ : Drive Forward
  • S or ↓ : Drive Backward
  • A or ← : Pivot Turn Left
  • D or → : Pivot Turn Right
  • SPACE or ESC : Immediate Emergency Stop
  • Key Release : Halts vehicle automatically

Safety Protocols

  • Car always powers up in STOP mode.
  • Watchdog shuts motors off if communication drops for 1.5 seconds.
  • Always elevate car wheels off the ground during physical bench testing.
  • Common ground (GND) must connect between Raspberry Pi and L298N.
